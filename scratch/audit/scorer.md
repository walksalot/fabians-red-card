# scorer

## SUMMARY
The 8-point first-goalscorer pick is free text with ZERO roster validation at any layer — the API route accepts any string up to 120 chars (src/app/api/picks/route.ts:12-18), the service only trims and length-caps at 80 (src/lib/services/picks.ts:11-12,66-77), and scoring passes the raw string straight into scorerMatches (src/lib/services/results.ts:345-356); the UI squad dropdown is a pure suggestion overlay on a free-text combobox (PickForm.tsx:361-388) and submits whatever was typed (PickForm.tsx:201). Running the verbatim matcher against the real data (48 squads, 1,246 players, 72 evaluable group fixtures of 104), a bare-surname pick covering 2+ players exists in 60 of 72 fixtures (83%); the best single pick covers a MEDIAN of 2 and MAX of 3 players per fixture vs exactly 1 for an honest full-name pick — strictly dominant, free, and explicitly legal under the published rules ('last names count', rules/page.tsx:266-268). The strongest strings are 'martinez' (Argentina: Emiliano/Lisandro/Lautaro — 3 players in 3 fixtures), 'sarr' and 'ndiaye' (Senegal, 3 outfielders each), 'suzuki' (Japan), 'pierre' (Haiti); 'rodriguez' hits in 15 of 72 fixtures. The worst structural twist: the dropdown inserts the FULL name (PickForm.tsx:425), so the app's default tap-to-pick flow locks honest users to 1-player coverage while a knowing player typing the bare surname gets up to 3x the hit probability on the second-largest scoring category (8 pts, 16 when boosted) — a systematic edge plausibly worth 10-25 expected points over the tournament, enough to reorder podium places in a 13-15 player, $600 pool.

## RISKS
- [high/high] Bare-surname picks strictly dominate full-name picks (up to 3x hit probability on the 8-point scorer category)
  evidence: src/lib/scoring.ts:95-104; src/lib/services/picks.ts:66-77; src/app/api/picks/route.ts:12-18; scratch/scorer-audit/results.json
  Mechanism: scorerMatches treats any whole-token suffix as a hit and nothing validates predScorer against rosters, so typing 'martinez' in an Argentina match is scored as a hit if ANY of Emiliano, Lisandro or Lautaro Martínez scores first — while the dropdown-guided honest pick 'Lautaro Martínez' covers exactly 1. A 2+ player cover exists in 60 of 72 known fixtures (median best cover 2, max 3). Who exploits it: any of the ~13-15 friends who notices; it is legal under the published rules and undetectable as cheating. Worth: covering 1-2 extra outfielders adds roughly 3-6% first-scorer hit probability per match, i.e. ~0.25-0.5 expected points per match (8 pts/category, 16 on the boosted match), compounding to ~10-25 expected points over the remaining ~100 matches — comparable to typical podium gaps in a 15-player pool and therefore plausibly worth a payout place ($360/$180/$60 of the $600 pot at the default 60/30/10 split). Tournament started today, so nearly the full edge is still ahead.
- [medium/high] Default UI flow penalizes honest users: dropdown inserts full names (coverage 1), typed surnames get coverage up to 3
  evidence: src/app/league/[slug]/_components/PickForm.tsx:389-443,425; src/app/league/[slug]/rules/page.tsx:266-268; src/lib/services/picks.ts:156-178
  The app's own happy path (tap a player in the squad list, PickForm.tsx:425 setScorer(n) with the full roster name) yields the minimum-coverage pick, while the rules page actively teaches that last names count without warning about collisions. Two players with identical intent ('I think a Martínez scores first') earn systematically different expected points based purely on whether they typed or tapped. This is a fairness defect even with zero malicious actors, and after the first kickoff reveal it splits the pool into early-knowers and late-knowers of the trick.
- [low/high] Roster data quirk: Brazil's 'Ederson'/'Éderson' normalize to the same key — one pick exact-matches two players
  evidence: src/lib/scoring.ts:74-82; data/rosters.json (BRA: 'Ederson' G, 'Éderson' M); scratch/scorer-audit/results.json fullNameCollisions
  normalizeName strips diacritics, so the two distinct Brazil players collapse to one identity; the pick 'ederson' scores if either the goalkeeper or the midfielder nets first, in every Brazil match. Same class of edge as the surname exploit but triggered by an exact-looking pick, so even a 'full name' here covers 2. Also one cross-team full-name twin exists (Emiliano Martínez, ARG and URU) but is harmless since matching is per-match. Small bounded edge; mostly a data-hygiene note for result entry (the admin-entered firstScorer string for either Ederson is indistinguishable).
- [low/high] Validation-bound mismatch: API accepts predScorer up to 120 chars, service rejects >80
  evidence: src/app/api/picks/route.ts:15; src/lib/services/picks.ts:12,70-75
  z.max(120) at the route vs MAX_SCORER_LENGTH=80 in the service — strings of 81-120 chars pass zod then 400 at the service. No fairness or money impact (the request fails safely), just an inconsistent boundary worth aligning whenever roster validation is added.

## FACTS
- (No roster validation at pick time) predScorer is free text: zod schema allows any string trimmed to max 120 chars with no roster check; the service layer only trims and rejects >80 chars (note the 80 vs 120 inconsistency); nothing ever compares the string to the players table.
  [src/app/api/picks/route.ts:12-18; src/lib/services/picks.ts:11-12,66-77,108]
- (No roster validation at scoring time) The stored predScorer string is passed raw into scorePick/scorerMatches when a result is entered — the players table (which exists, schema.ts:84-94) is never consulted by validation or scoring; it only feeds the UI dropdown.
  [src/lib/services/results.ts:345-356; src/lib/scoring.ts:122-127; src/db/schema.ts:84-94; src/app/league/[slug]/today/page.tsx:76-89,205-206]
- (UI is free text, dropdown is a suggestion only) PickForm's scorer field is a type=text combobox; the squad list merely filters as you type and an option tap inserts the FULL player name; any typed value is submitted as-is. The prop comment confirms: 'empty arrays fall back to free text only'.
  [src/app/league/[slug]/_components/PickForm.tsx:17,201,361-388,389-443,425]
- (Rules advertise surname matching) The rules page tells players 'last names count ("Jimenez" = "Raúl Jiménez")' and 'Spelling, accents, capitalization, and periods are forgiven' — so a bare-surname pick is explicitly legal, but the collision consequence (one string covering 3 players) is never disclosed.
  [src/app/league/[slug]/rules/page.tsx:73,266-268]
- (Matcher semantics) scorerMatches awards the points when the normalized pick equals the actual scorer's normalized name OR is a whole-token suffix of it with strictly fewer tokens — so 'martinez' (1 token) matches every player whose normalized name ends in 'martinez'.
  [src/lib/scoring.ts:74-82,95-104]
- (Collision census (real rosters)) 27 of 48 teams have at least one colliding surname; 44 colliding surname groups covering 93 players. Five surnames cover 3 players on one squad: ARG 'martinez' (Emiliano G, Lisandro D, Lautaro F), HAI 'pierre' (G,M,M), JPN 'suzuki' (G,D,M), SEN 'sarr' (D,M,F) and SEN 'ndiaye' (M,F,F).
  [scratch/scorer-audit/audit.mjs (verbatim matcher copy, lines 16-37); scratch/scorer-audit/results.json]
- (Per-fixture exploitability) Across the 72 group fixtures with known rosters (32 knockout slots are still TBD), the best single pick string covers: 1 player in 12 fixtures, 2 players in 48 fixtures, 3 players in 12 fixtures — median 2, mean 2.0, max 3. 136 distinct (pick,fixture) pairs cover >=2 players.
  [scratch/scorer-audit/results.json honestVsExploiter]
- (Top exploitable fixtures) Matches 5/29/50 (Haiti games, 'pierre'=3), 11/36/57 (Japan, 'suzuki'=3), 17/41 (Senegal, 'sarr'=3, plus 'ndiaye'=3 in match 17), 19/43 (Argentina, 'martinez'=3) — all three-player covers; both Senegal trios are all-outfield, maximizing real probability gain.
  [scratch/scorer-audit/results.json top10ExploitableFixtures]
- (Cross-team picks) Four fixtures have a single pick string covering players on BOTH squads (paying whichever side scores first): 'zakaria' (Qatar v Switzerland, m8), 'ayari' (Sweden v Tunisia, m12), 'ahmed' (Canada v Qatar, m27), 'varela' (Uruguay v Cabo Verde, m37).
  [scratch/scorer-audit/audit.mjs; verified output in transcript]
- (Full-name collisions (reverse check)) One cross-team identical normalized full name: 'emiliano martinez' on both ARG and URU (harmless — matching is always against the actual scorer of that match). One same-team duplicate: Brazil lists both 'Ederson' and 'Éderson', which normalize identically, so the pick 'ederson' exact-matches two different Brazil players (GK and MF) in every Brazil fixture.
  [scratch/scorer-audit/results.json fullNameCollisions; data/rosters.json BRA entries]
- (Exploit visibility spreads after kickoff) All picks become public at kickoff, so the first time someone's bare 'martinez' pick lands on the board, the trick is visible and copyable by the rest of the pool mid-tournament — creating a knowledge-timing asymmetry rather than a stable secret.
