# tiebreak

## SUMMARY
Tiebreaker and booster mechanics verified to the line. (1) TIEBREAKER: confirmed — lastPickAt = MAX(picks.updatedAt) over ALL of an entry's picks (leaderboard.ts:99-103), upsertPick unconditionally refreshes updatedAt even on a byte-identical no-op re-save (picks.ts:128-137, no equality check), and compareStandings sorts that key ASCENDING (leaderboard.ts:52-54), so the entry whose most recent pick activity is OLDEST wins ties; final fallback is entryId asc = earliest signup (leaderboard.ts:55), which is undocumented in the UI. Because knockout picks cannot be pre-saved while teams are TBD (picks.ts:104-106), every player is forced to save late-tournament picks, making the tiebreak effectively "who finished entering their last pick a few minutes earlier" — noise worth up to $180 (1st vs 2nd of the ~$600 pot) — and it actively penalizes reacting to lineup news with a pick edit while booster moves cost nothing. The rules page documents the direction ("whoever last saved a pick earlier wins... Decisiveness pays", rules/page.tsx:203-206) but not the no-op-save gotcha or the entryId fallback. (2) BOOSTER: the full timing lattice is kickoff-only — set/move requires target unkicked (boosters.ts:58-60) and previous holder unkicked (boosters.ts:88-90), clear requires holder unkicked (boosters.ts:183-185); match.status is never checked, and the code explicitly recomputes points when the target or previous match is already 'finished' (boosters.ts:79-80,91,127-129,187-192). This is asymmetric with picks, which lock the moment a result exists even before kickoff "because the result is public" (picks.ts:98-103) — so if a result is ever entered ahead of kickoff, players can place a booster on a known result (guaranteed multiplied points) or pull it off a known bust. Waiting until the last pre-kickoff moment to place the booster is weakly dominant (only downside is forgetting), and the 20s-polling live provisional board (LiveNow.tsx) plus staggered kickoffs let players condition placement/removal on same-day results; no UI nudges about timing exist — the rules even reassure "it can never cost you anything" (rules/page.tsx:239-241). (3) Badges/streaks are purely cosmetic, rendered only on the profile page. (4) Float totals are stored as REAL and summed in row order, but micro-rounding to 1e-6 before comparison (leaderboard.ts:44-46,112; today-points.ts:70) safely absorbs worst-case ~1e-9 addend-order drift; a regression test covers it (leaderboard.test.ts:201).

## RISKS
- [medium/high] Booster can be placed on (or pulled off) a match whose result is already known, whenever a result lands before the recorded kickoff
  evidence: src/lib/services/boosters.ts:58-60,78-92,99-131,180-193; src/lib/services/picks.ts:98-103; src/lib/services/results.ts:341-368
  Mechanism: picks lock the instant a result exists (picks.ts treats early results as public), but booster set/move/clear gates ONLY on kickoff time and deliberately handles finished targets via the recompute path. If the admin ever enters a result ahead of kickoffUtc (correction, abandoned/rescheduled match, or a stale kickoff timestamp while the sync marks the match finished), any player watching can move their daily booster onto a match where their pick already scored — a guaranteed x2 (default boosterMultiplier) on known points — or vacate a boosted bust and re-place on a live alternative. Worth up to one full boosted match score (e.g., exact 10 + scorer 8 + firstTeam 2 = 20 -> +20 bonus points at x2), easily enough to flip podium places in a 13-15 player pool paying $360/$180/$60. Exploitable by any player who notices the early result; symmetric in theory but in practice rewards whoever is online at the right moment. Fix sketch: mirror the pick gate — reject set/move/clear when the target or holder match has status 'finished'.
- [medium/high] Money-deciding tiebreak is last-save-timestamp noise that punishes legitimate play and counts no-op saves
  evidence: src/lib/services/leaderboard.ts:48-56,74-78,99-103; src/lib/services/picks.ts:104-106,115,128-137; src/app/league/[slug]/rules/page.tsx:197-207
  lastPickAt = max(updatedAt) over ALL picks, refreshed by ANY save including a byte-identical re-save (upsertPick has no change detection), compared ascending. Because knockout picks cannot be pre-filled (TBD teams 409), every player must save late, so ties at the end are decided by who happened to finish typing their last pick minutes earlier — and a player who merely re-opened the form and hit Save without changing anything silently drops behind. It also directly penalizes improving a pick after lineup news, while booster moves carry no such cost. With a ~$600 pot at 60/30/10, a top-2 tie decided this way swings $180. The rule is partially documented ('Decisiveness pays') but the no-op-save behavior, the all-picks-tournament-wide scope, and the entryId-ascending final fallback (earliest signup wins; a user's entry #1 beats their entry #2) are not disclosed anywhere players can see. Fix sketch: skip the updatedAt refresh when values are unchanged; consider scoping the tiebreak to picks on tied-relevant matches or replacing it with goal-difference-style criteria; document the entryId fallback.
- [low/high] Last-moment booster placement is weakly dominant and conditionable on same-day information, favoring always-online players
  evidence: src/lib/services/boosters.ts:55-92,180-193; src/app/league/[slug]/rules/page.tsx:149-160,233-241; src/app/league/[slug]/_components/LiveNow.tsx:8,27-45
  Mechanism: there is zero cost or reward attached to placement time, so the optimal strategy is to never commit before a kickoff boundary forces you to — keep the booster unset, watch early kickoffs and the 20s-polling 'if it ended now' board, then at each kickoff decide only whether the next-to-kick match is your boost; this strictly weakly dominates morning placement (only operational risk is forgetting, forfeiting the day's booster). It also enables variance-seeking: after early-game busts, move/clear a booster sitting on a not-yet-kicked match and re-place on the pick with the fattest tail. All of this is by-design 'movable until kickoff' semantics and symmetric on paper, but in a friends pool it converts the booster from a commitment mechanic into an attention tax — players who set-and-forget (as the 'choose wisely' copy implies) systematically donate option value to players who babysit the app. Expected edge is modest (a few points per matchday of improved boost EV) but compounds over 100+ matches. Fix sketch if unwanted: lock the day's booster at the matchday's FIRST kickoff, or disclose the dominant strategy in the rules so everyone plays it.
- [low/high] Leaderboard API exposes every entry's lastPickAt, enabling tiebreak surveillance
  evidence: src/app/api/leagues/[slug]/leaderboard/route.ts:23,31-36; src/lib/services/leaderboard.ts:20-22; src/app/league/[slug]/_components/LiveTable.tsx:27,374-409
  The leaderboard route spreads the full LeaderboardRow (including lastPickAt) to all league members, and the client polls it every 30s. A player contending for a tied payout can watch rivals' lastPickAt in real time and manage their own save timing to stay 'older' (save final picks once, never touch the form again, and know exactly when a rival's edit hands them the tie). Pick contents stay hidden, but the timestamp itself is the tiebreak asset; publishing it asymmetrically rewards whoever realizes it's there. Worth up to the same $180 1st/2nd swing as the tiebreak itself. Fix sketch: strip lastPickAt from the serialized rows (the UI never renders it).

## FACTS
- (tiebreak key derivation) lastPickAt is computed as the maximum picks.updatedAt over ALL of an entry's picks (every pick row for the entry, past and future matchdays alike), null when the entry has no picks.
  [src/lib/services/leaderboard.ts:74-78,99-103,116]
- (tiebreak sort direction) compareStandings order: total desc, exactCount desc, scorerHits desc, then lastPickAt ASCENDING (smaller/older epoch wins; null coerces to Infinity so pickless entries sort last on this key), then entryId ascending. Worked example: A and B both {total:120, exact:4, scorer:3}; A.lastPickAt=1000, B.lastPickAt=2000 -> compare returns 1000-2000=-1000 <0 -> A sorts first and takes the higher rank. The entry whose MOST RECENT pick save is OLDEST wins the tie. Unit test confirms direction.
  [src/lib/services/leaderboard.ts:48-56,120-121; tests/unit/leaderboard.test.ts:181-198]
- (no-op saves refresh the tiebreak) upsertPick's onConflictDoUpdate always sets updatedAt=nowMs() with no comparison against existing values — re-saving an identical pick (or tapping Save twice) worsens the entry's tiebreak position. The PickForm save button is enabled whenever not mid-save (disabled only on status==='saving'), so identical re-saves are easy to trigger.
  [src/lib/services/picks.ts:115,128-137; src/app/league/[slug]/_components/PickForm.tsx:509-512]
- (tiebreak is forced into noise by knockout TBD lock) Picks cannot be saved while either team is null ('Teams for this match are not set yet', 409), so nobody can pre-fill knockout picks; every active player necessarily saves picks late in the tournament, so the last tiebreak in practice compares minutes-level timestamps of final-round pick entry — 'who typed faster/earlier on the last day' — rather than any skill signal. It also penalizes editing a pick after lineup news (~1h pre-kickoff) since any edit refreshes max(updatedAt).
  [src/lib/services/picks.ts:104-106; src/lib/services/leaderboard.ts:52-54]
- (booster moves are tiebreak-free, pick edits are not) Only picks.updatedAt feeds lastPickAt; boosters.updatedAt is never read by the leaderboard. Reacting to news by moving a booster costs nothing, while reacting by editing a pick damages tiebreak position — inconsistent incentive design.
  [src/lib/services/leaderboard.ts:74-78; src/lib/services/boosters.ts:111]
- (UI explanation of the tiebreak) Rules page lists 4 tiebreakers ending with 'Earliest pick submission — whoever last saved a pick earlier wins the tie. Decisiveness pays.' It omits (a) that identical no-op re-saves count, (b) that the key is the max over ALL picks tournament-wide, and (c) the final entryId-ascending fallback (earliest signup / a user's first entry beats their second). LiveTable shows only 'Tied at the top with X — tiebreakers decide'.
  [src/app/league/[slug]/rules/page.tsx:197-207; src/app/league/[slug]/_components/LiveTable.tsx:131,139; src/lib/services/leaderboard.ts:55]
- (booster timing lattice) setBooster: target must be on the requested matchday (400), unkicked (409 at clock>=kickoff), teams non-null (409); moving additionally requires the current holder match unkicked (409 'Booster already locked'). clearBooster: allowed until the holder match kicks off. All gates are kickoff-time only; match.status is never a gate. If the old or new match is already status='finished' (admin early result), recomputeMatch is invoked inside the same transaction so multiplied/unmultiplied points commit atomically with the booster write.
  [src/lib/services/boosters.ts:55-65,78-92,99-131,180-193]
- (booster vs pick lock asymmetry on early results) upsertPick rejects picks once match.status==='finished' even before kickoff, with the comment 'from that moment the result is public'. setBooster/clearBooster have no such status check and explicitly support targeting or vacating a finished-but-unkicked match (the finishedMatchIds recompute path exists precisely for this).
  [src/lib/services/picks.ts:98-103; src/lib/services/boosters.ts:58-60,79-80,91]
- (booster recompute applies multiplier to existing pick) recomputeMatchForLeague re-scores every pick on the match and applies boosted=true for entries whose booster row points at the match, multiplying by league.boosterMultiplier — so a booster placed after a result is known multiplies an already-determined score.
  [src/lib/services/results.ts:316-368]
- (no UI timing nudges for the booster) Rules copy says 'choose wisely' and 'A booster only multiplies points you earn — it can never cost you anything'; the Today header shows 'Booster available' and DayNav shows 'booster armed'. Nothing advises early commitment or warns that waiting is strictly better; the no-downside framing if anything encourages last-moment placement.
  [src/app/league/[slug]/rules/page.tsx:149-160,233-241; src/app/league/[slug]/today/page.tsx:239; src/app/league/[slug]/_components/DayNav.tsx:147]
- (badges/streaks are cosmetic) getEntryStats (accuracy, currentStreak, bestStreak, badges like 'Sniper'/'Hot Streak'/'Ever Present') is consumed only by the profile page; nothing in standings ranking, payouts, or any API ordering reads badges or streaks. Streak counts any matchPoints total>0 on finished picked matches in kickoff order.
  [src/lib/services/leaderboard.ts:125-209; src/app/league/[slug]/profile/page.tsx:154,379-387]
- (float totals and micro-rounding) matchPoints.total is a REAL computed deterministically per pick (results.ts), so two entries with identical per-match histories get bit-identical addends; only summation order differs across entries. roundTotal(value)=Math.round(value*1e6)/1e6 is applied to the season sum before compareStandings and to today-points display. Worst-case addend-order drift for ~104 matches at realistic magnitudes is ~1e-12 (even with the admin-max boosterMultiplier of 100, ~1e-9), far below the 5e-7 rounding threshold — so mathematically equal totals cannot diverge in practice, and genuinely different totals under default integer scoring differ by >=1 (or >=0.000001 with exotic multipliers) so are not falsely merged. A regression test pins this.

- (live provisional board does not use the tiebreak) The 'if it ended now' per-match boards sort rows by has-pick, provisional total desc, then label localeCompare — lastPickAt plays no role in the live view; it only bites in banked standings.
