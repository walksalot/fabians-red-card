The one Critical

The winner screen pays out a pot nobody agreed to

The payout reads the live setup draft instead of a snapshot taken when the game started. So if someone opens “New game”, fiddles with the buy-in, and backs out without starting — then resumes the game already in progress — the win screen settles up using the abandoned draft.

Verified in reverse too: a real agreed $2/head to @realaunt became “$12 · send it to @some-other-cousin” — a scannable Venmo QR, for the wrong amount, to someone who was never in the game.

The skeptic attacked this on four axes and it survived all of them, including the strongest defence: the app's own saved stake said enabled:false at the moment the screen displayed $40. Root cause is named in shipped code — payoutFor() in ui.js reads view.setup.buyin rather than a per-game snapshot.

Why it rates Critical: the rubric calls wrong money math S1 outright, and this lands on the exact screen the room uses to settle up.

Reveal's 15s auto-advance is only cancelled by taps on [data-action] controls; taps on the card/verdict are ignored, and the one cancelling tap (a vote chip) flips the recorded vote
repro & verdict

Expected Per the shipped fast-flow contract ('any other interaction cancels the countdown'), any tap on the reveal cancels the auto-advance without side effects, and the screen stays until Next player is tapped.

Actual Only clicks landing on a [data-action] control cancel (ui.js global click handler ~4428); taps on the card face, year, artist, artwork, banner, or reveal strip leave the countdown draining and the screen self-advances at 15s - and the only non-Next controls that do cancel are the vote chips, whose tap mutates the vote and immediately re-arms a fresh countdown.

  1. Open https://music-timeline-walksalots-projects.vercel.app/index.html?debug=1&seed=555 as a fresh visitor (empty localStorage), fast flow on (default)
  2. New game -> Skip photo on every row -> Shuffle & start
  3. On the pass screen tap the continue button (card draws, song starts)
  4. Tap any gap, tap Place here
  5. On the reveal, while the Next player button shows its draining fill (~3s in), tap the revealed card face (#reveal-flip) or the verdict banner - not a button
  6. Wait out the remaining ~12s: the app auto-advances to the pass screen anyway
  7. Variant (seed 44004, Advanced mode, vote both chips first): tapping a vote chip to 'pause' does cancel-and-rearm, but the group's recorded vote flips (banner changes from 'Correct. The card is yours.' to 'Right spot, but you had to name it too.')
