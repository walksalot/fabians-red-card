The score went up, but the app did not get worse. Round 1's 25 fixes held — skeptics re-tested those areas and none regressed. The rise is reach: 29 of 35 findings are minor polish, and this round reached surfaces round 1 never touched (co-op wording, offline and storage-full behaviour, landscape, the listen-page QR, screen-reader labels). Round 1 found the big breaks; round 2 is finding the long tail.

The one Critical

The winner screen pays out a pot nobody agreed to

The payout reads the live setup draft instead of a snapshot taken when the game started. So if someone opens “New game”, fiddles with the buy-in, and backs out without starting — then resumes the game already in progress — the win screen settles up using the abandoned draft.

Verified in reverse too: a real agreed $2/head to @realaunt became “$12 · send it to @some-other-cousin” — a scannable Venmo QR, for the wrong amount, to someone who was never in the game.

The skeptic attacked this on four axes and it survived all of them, including the strongest defence: the app's own saved stake said enabled:false at the moment the screen displayed $40. Root cause is named in shipped code — payoutFor() in ui.js reads view.setup.buyin rather than a per-game snapshot.

Why it rates Critical: the rubric calls wrong money math S1 outright, and this lands on the exact screen the room uses to settle up.

Advanced/expert group vote cannot be completed as instructed: partial votes strand the verdict, toggling flashes a false 'Correct' verdict with win cue, and a live Next silently forfeits a correctly placed card
repro & verdict

Expected The group can record 'they got it wrong' as instructed; no verdict, win cue, or scoreboard change renders until the vote settles; Next is blocked (or clearly announces the forfeit) while the vote is open.

Actual The confirmations model is tri-state with no direct path to No: a single tap leaves the other field null so the verdict strands at 'Waiting'; tapping a chip ON makes both fields non-null and immediately renders acceptance (false 'Correct' banner + win sfx) until the OFF tap corrects it; btn-next-player is never disabled while the vote is open, so tapping it resolves nulls as unconfirmed and silently discards the correctly placed card.

S2
'Play again' clears the finished game's saved result at tap time, so a reload during post-game setup permanently loses the winner screen and payout QR while the pot may still be unsettled
repro & verdict

Expected The finished game's recap and pot stay reachable until a NEW game actually starts — the app's own persist() rationale is that finished games are saved precisely because switching to Venmo to settle reloads the tab.

Actual The play-again handler runs state=null; clearGame(); removeStored(ACTIVE_BUYIN_KEY) immediately on tap and only then shows setup; any reload from the setup screen lands on a bare home with winner, pot amount, and payout QR permanently gone.

  1. Fresh page at https://music-timeline-walksalots-projects.vercel.app/index.html?debug=1&seed=42424: New game; Skip photo on all rows; First to 5; Buy-in ON; enter a Venmo handle; Shuffle & start

  5. Land on home: no 'See final result' card, no resume — the saved game key and the buy-in snapshot were removed at Play-again tap time, before any new game started
