                      How to play

                      Be the first to build a timeline of correctly ordered songs. You never see a year until you have committed to a spot.

                      Setting up

                      • Two to eight players. Enter everyone's name on the setup screen.
                      • Everyone starts with one song card face up in their timeline, year showing, and 2 tokens. In co-op there is one starting card and one pool of 2 tokens for the whole table.
                      • The rest of the songs become a shared face-down deck.
                      • Choose a target of 5, 10 or 15 cards, a mode, and which decades and genres are in play.

                      A turn

                      1. The phone is passed to the active player. Everyone else looks away.
                      2. They draw the top card and play the 30-second preview. The year is hidden. Anyone can scan the QR code to listen on their own phone — that page never shows the year either.
                      3. They pick a gap in their own timeline: before the earliest card, after the latest, or between any two. Tap the gap, then tap Place here.
                      4. The card flips. Right spot → it joins their timeline. Wrong spot → it is discarded.
                      5. Play passes to the left.

                      Ties are kind: if the song came out in the same year as a card you already hold, either side of that card counts as correct.

                      Tokens

                      You start with 2 and can never hold more than 5. There are three things to spend them on.

                      Challenge — 1 token

                      • Before the reveal, any player other than the active one may challenge.
                      • You spend a token and nominate a gap in your own timeline.
                      • At the reveal the active player is resolved first. If they put the card in the wrong spot and you picked a right one, the card goes to you.
                      • If several challengers were right, the card goes to the one closest to the active player's left.
                      • A wrong challenge just costs the token. Challenging a correct placement is allowed — it also just costs the token.
                      • Only the spot counts here. In advanced and expert, a card that was placed correctly but lost on the title, artist or year is discarded rather than won — challengers get nothing.
                      • One challenge per player per turn.

                      Name that song — earn 1 token

                      • During your turn, tap I can name it if you can give the title and the artist.
                      • At the reveal the group votes with the Title and Artist buttons.
                      • Get both and you gain a token, whether or not the placement was right.

                      Buy a card — 3 tokens

                      • Only at the start of your turn, before you have heard the song.
                      • The top card is dropped into the correct spot for you. No guessing, no risk.
                      • Your turn then ends.

                      Winning

                      • The first player to reach the target number of cards wins, as soon as that turn ends.
                      • If the deck runs out first, the longest timeline wins.
                      • Still tied? Most tokens wins. Still tied after that, the win is shared.
                      • Co-op is all or nothing: the table wins by reaching the target together, and loses if it hits the mistake limit or empties the deck first.

                      The four modes

                      Classic
                      Placement only. The friendliest version and the right one for a mixed-age room.
                      Advanced
                      The placement only counts if you also name the title and the artist. The group confirms both at the reveal.
                      Expert
                      Advanced, plus you must call the exact release year with the year stepper before you place. Exact match or nothing.
                      Co-op
                      One shared timeline and one shared token pool. Everyone still takes turns placing, but you are all on the same side. Reach the target together and you win; hit the mistake limit (3 by default) and the game is over for everybody.

                      Tokens are spent and earned the same way in every mode. The only difference is whose they are: in co-op there is one pool, so a challenge or a bought card is paid for by the whole table.

                      House notes

                      • Song previews need internet. Everything else works offline.
                      • The pass-the-phone screen exists so nobody sees the previous reveal. You can switch it off in the menu.
                      • If a preview will not load, skip the card. No penalty.
